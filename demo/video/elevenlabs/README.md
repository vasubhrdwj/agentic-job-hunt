# ElevenLabs narration

Paste the complete contents of `../elevenlabs-full-narration.txt` into
ElevenLabs Text to Speech and download one continuous MP3 or WAV file.

Save it here as:

```text
full-narration.mp3
```

Keep the blank-line paragraph pauses. They let the video renderer align the
product, Phoenix trace, comparison chart, and closing visuals to the narration.
